# Nuvora Promo — Vevara Build Guide

Vevara works in "Moments" — one scene at a time, each with its own duration, that you edit like a Canva slide (Elements/Frames/Bg wall/Audio/Text/Uploads on the left, a Moments list on the right, timeline at the bottom). This maps the existing shot list onto that structure so you can build it Moment-by-Moment.

**Do this first — record the raw screen footage.** Vevara can't generate the [UI] shots itself; you upload real video into a Moment. Screen-record nuvora.ps (QuickTime → File → New Screen Recording, or Cmd+Shift+5) for each of these before opening Vevara, so you're not context-switching mid-build:

1. Dashboard — slow zoom into the task/goal/habit summary cards
2. Tasks board, Goals with milestones, Calendar, Habit streaks — one continuous cursor-driven pass, ~2s each
3. Lumi chat — typing "I have an exam Thursday, help me plan my week", sending, response streaming in, action chip confirming
4. Focus timer starting + the tree growing
5. Analytics chart, Projects board, CV builder, Internship tracker — quick pass, ~0.8s each

Trim each clip to roughly the length noted per Moment below before uploading — Vevara can still adjust duration in the timeline, but starting close cuts down on fiddling.

## Moment-by-Moment

**Moment 1 — The Problem (0:00–0:06)**
Upload: 3–4 short stock/phone clips (notifications, cluttered notes, red-deadline calendar, half-finished list) — either as one pre-cut sequence or as 3–4 back-to-back short Moments (~1.5s each) so the cuts are real cuts, not crossfades.
Text (appears on the last cut only): "Student life isn't just assignments."
No VO.

**Moment 2 — The Insight (0:06–0:11)**
Background: the same footage, slowed and color-graded warmer toward lavender (Vevara's Bg wall / filter controls, or pre-grade before upload if it doesn't have a warmth slider).
Text, one phrase at a time with a soft cross-fade, ~1s hold each: "Classes." → "Goals." → "Habits." → "Exams." → "Your future."
VO: "It's all connected — even when it doesn't feel that way."

**Moment 3 — The Turn (0:11–0:12)**
Background: solid lavender gradient (Bg wall).
Element: Nuvora logo mark, small, centered, fades in.
Text: "Meet Nuvora."
No VO — let it breathe.

**Moment 4 — The Big Idea (0:12–0:19)**
Upload: Dashboard recording. Use Vevara's morph/zoom transition into this Moment from Moment 3's logo (not a hard cut) if it supports cross-Moment transitions — otherwise a quick fade.
VO (longest single line — don't rush it): "A Life OS, built for your entire student life — not just your to-do list."

**Moment 5 — Core Experience (0:19–0:28)**
Upload: the Tasks/Goals/Calendar/Habits continuous recording, ~2s per screen within it.
Text, small lower-third, one word timed to each screen: "Plan." / "Achieve." / "Track." / "Build."
No VO — music carries it.

**Moment 6 — Lumi (0:28–0:36)**
Upload: the Lumi chat recording.
VO: "And when you don't know where to start — Lumi figures it out with you."
Hold the last frame (action-chip confirmation) a beat before the Moment ends.

**Moment 7 — Flow (0:36–0:42)**
Upload: focus timer + tree-growing recording, intercut once with a warm student-studying shot if you have one.
Text: "Protect your focus."
VO: "Then, protect your focus — and watch it grow."
Give this Moment a touch more time than its 6s if the timeline allows — it's meant to be the most visually distinct beat.

**Moment 8 — Big Picture Montage (0:42–0:47)**
Upload: Analytics/Projects/CV/Internships recording, ~0.8s per screen.
Text, single small word per screen: "Grow." / "Build." / "Prepare."
No VO — keep it quick, this is proof-of-breadth only.

**Moment 9 — Brand Ending (0:47–0:52)**
Background: fade from the last UI frame to the lavender gradient.
Element: Nuvora wordmark, scales in gently, no hard snap.
Text: "NUVORA.PS" → beat → "Your student life. One system."
VO (spoken as the tagline lands, same words as on-screen — the one deliberate overlap): "Your student life. One system."

## Audio
Upload the 5 VO lines as one audio track (Vevara's Audio panel / "Upload audio from device") once recorded, then nudge each Moment's duration in the timeline so the cuts land on the VO beats rather than fighting them. Pick a music bed that builds gradually rather than hitting hard on every cut, since the visual pace here is deliberately slower than Vevara's default templates assume — lower the music under the VO Moments, let it carry Moments 5 and 8 where there's no VO.

## Before you export
- Every [UI] Moment should be real nuvora.ps footage, not the stock template art — swap out anything still showing the default "Best Company / Smart Lifestyle App" placeholder.
- Stay inside Nuvora's lavender/purple gradient — don't pull in a template's default palette for the background Moments.
- Total runtime target: ~48–52s. Vevara's timeline will show your running total — check it against this before exporting.
