# Nuvora Promo — Shot-by-Shot Script
**Target runtime:** ~48–52s · **Style:** kinetic typography (reference clip), slowed ~30-40%, Nuvora's own palette instead of the reference's blue/red · **VO:** sparse — 5 lines only, everything else carried by on-screen text + UI

Legend: **[VO]** = voiceover line · **[TEXT]** = on-screen kinetic typography (no VO under it) · **[UI]** = real screen-recorded app footage, not a mockup

---

### 0:00–0:06 — THE PROBLEM
**[UI/stock]** Fast but not frantic cuts: a phone lighting up with notifications, a cluttered notes app, a calendar full of red deadline blocks, a half-finished to-do list. 3-4 cuts, ~1.5s each — chaotic but each shot readable, not a blur.
**[TEXT, appears over the last cut]** "Student life isn't just assignments."
No VO yet — let the visual chaos speak first.

### 0:06–0:11 — THE INSIGHT
**[TEXT, one phrase at a time, soft cross-fade between each, ~1s hold each]**
"Classes." → "Goals." → "Habits." → "Exams." → "Your future."
Background: the chaotic cuts from section 1 start slowing down and softening, color grading warms toward Nuvora's lavender.
**[VO #1]** "It's all connected — even when it doesn't feel that way."

### 0:11–0:12 — THE TURN
Screen fades to soft lavender gradient (Nuvora's brand gradient, not a hard color block). Logo mark fades in, small, centered.
**[TEXT]** "Meet Nuvora."
Beat of near-silence here — let it breathe before the reveal.

### 0:12–0:19 — THE BIG IDEA
**[UI]** Smooth morph-transition (not a cut) from the logo into the actual Dashboard, full screen. Slow, confident zoom into the dashboard's task/goal/habit summary cards.
**[VO #2]** "A Life OS, built for your entire student life — not just your to-do list."
This is the most important line in the video — give it the longest single VO breath, don't rush it.

### 0:19–0:28 — THE CORE EXPERIENCE
**[UI]** One continuous-feeling sequence, smooth UI-to-UI morph transitions (not hard cuts) between:
Tasks board → Goals w/ milestones → Calendar → Habit streaks
~2s per screen. Cursor visibly interacting (dragging a task, checking a habit) so it reads as alive, not a slideshow.
**[TEXT, small lower-third, one word per screen]** "Plan." / "Achieve." / "Track." / "Build."
No VO here — let the UI demo itself. Music carries the energy.

### 0:28–0:36 — AI: LUMI (live demo beat)
**[UI]** Cut to Lumi's chat interface. Real screen recording:
1. Cursor clicks the input field ("Ask Lumi anything…")
2. Text types in naturally (not instant-paste) — something concrete and relatable, e.g. "I have an exam Thursday, help me plan my week"
3. Send. Brief thinking state.
4. Response streams in, ending with a visible action chip (e.g. "✅ Task created") — proving Lumi actually *does* things, not just chats.
**[VO #3]** "And when you don't know where to start — Lumi figures it out with you."
Hold on the action-chip confirmation for a beat before cutting — that's the payoff of the whole shot.

### 0:36–0:42 — FLOW (the memorable one)
**[UI]** Focus timer starts, tree begins growing (use the actual Flow tree animation). Cut to a soft, warm environment shot (student studying, headphones on) intercut once with the tree mid-growth.
**[TEXT]** "Protect your focus."
**[VO #4]** "Then, protect your focus — and watch it grow."
This should visually be the most beautiful/distinctive beat in the whole video — give it slightly more breathing room than other sections even though it's short.

### 0:42–0:47 — BIG PICTURE MONTAGE
**[UI]** Quick but smooth (not rushed) montage, ~0.8s per screen: Analytics chart → Projects board → CV builder → Internship tracker.
**[TEXT, single word per screen, small]** "Grow." / "Build." / "Prepare."
No VO — this is proof-of-breadth, kept deliberately quick so it doesn't overstay.

### 0:47–0:52 — BRAND ENDING
**[UI→TEXT]** Smooth fade from app UI to clean lavender-gradient background. Nuvora wordmark centers, scales in gently (no hard snap).
**[TEXT]** "NUVORA.PS"
**[TEXT, beat after]** "Your student life. One system."
**[VO #5]** "Your student life. One system." *(spoken as the tagline lands on screen — the only place VO and on-screen text say the exact same words, on purpose, for emphasis)*

## Production notes
- **Total VO lines: 5** (roughly 25-30 words total) — short enough to record cleanly in one take, easy to re-voice in Arabic later if you want a bilingual cut.
- **Transitions:** morphs/wipes/cross-fades throughout, no hard cuts except in the opening chaos beat (0:00–0:06) where a little roughness is the point.
- **Color:** stay inside Nuvora's existing lavender/purple gradient system — don't invent new brand colors for this video, per your own brief section 11.
- **Music:** should carry more of the energy than the reference clip's transitions do, since we've deliberately slowed the visual pace down — pick a track that builds gradually rather than hits hard on every cut.
- **All [UI] shots should be real screen recordings of the live app** (nuvora.ps), not static mockups or after-effects UI recreations — this is a real, live, working product, and that authenticity is worth more than a slicker fake.
